Burn & care instructions
  • First burn: let the wax melt all the way to the edge (2–3 hours) to prevent tunneling.
  • Trim the wick to 5mm before every burn.
  • Burn for no more than 4 hours at a time.
  • Keep away from drafts, children and pets. Never leave a burning candle unattended.
  • Reed diffuser: flip the reeds once a week to refresh the scent.
